


  | From      | To          | Notes                  |
  |-----------|-------------|------------------------|
  | 12V PSU + | TMC2209 VM+ | Red wire recommended   |
  | 12V PSU - | TMC2209 GND | Black wire recommended |

  ESP32 to TMC2209 (Control Signals)

  | ESP32 Pin | TMC2209 Pin | Wire Color | Function                 |
  |-----------|-------------|------------|--------------------------|
    GPIO 12     tx            brown         PDN
    GPIO 13     RX              purple      UART
  | GPIO 25   | STEP        | Yellow     | Step pulses              |
  | GPIO 26   | DIR         | Green      | Direction control        |
  | GPIO 27   | EN          | Blue       | Enable (active LOW)      |
  | GND       | GND         | Black      | Common ground (CRITICAL) |

  TMC2209 to Motor

  | TMC2209Pin | Motor Connection | Notes            |Wire Colo
  |------------|------------------|------------------|
  | B2         | Coil 1 - Wire 1  | First coil pair  |. black
  | B1         | Coil 1 - Wire 2  | First coil pair  | greeen 
  | A1         | Coil 2 - Wire 1  | Second coil pair | red 
  | A2         | Coil 2 - Wire 2  | Second coil pair | blue



    Gets Added Later:

  Phase 2 (UART control):
  GPIO 16 (12)-----> TX (TMC2209 RX)
  GPIO 17 (13) -----> RX (TMC2209 TX)

  Phase 3 (Hall sensor):
  Hall Sensor    ESP32
  -----------    -----
  VCC ---------> 3.3V
  GND ---------> GND
  OUT ---------> GPIO 2 (hall sensor input)

  Recommendation:

  Since you're prototyping, I'd suggest:
  1. Temp solder Phase 1 connections now (STEP/DIR/EN/GND) - these won't change
  2. Keep Phase 2 UART wires loose for now - easier to debug if UART communication has issues
  3. Add Phase 3 hall sensor later when you get there

  The core motor control signals (STEP/DIR/EN) are solid and won't need to be changed, so soldering those makes sense for a more reliable connection during testing.
