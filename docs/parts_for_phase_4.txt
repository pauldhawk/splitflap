  Required Components

  Shift Registers:
  Output Control (Motor STEP/DIR signals):
  - 2× 74HC595 8-bit shift register (~$0.50 each)
    - Controls 12 bits (6 motors × 2 bits each)
    - Daisy-chained together

  Input Reading (Hall sensors):
  - 1× 74HC165 8-bit shift register (~$0.60 each)
    - Reads 6 sensor inputs

  Other Components:
  - 6× TMC2209 driver boards ($3-8 each, depending on source)
  - 6× NEMA 17 motors (you already have tested with 1)
  - 6× Hall effect sensors (you already have tested with 1)
  - 1× 12-24V power supply (6-10A capacity)
  - 3× 10kΩ resistor arrays (for pull-ups/pull-downs)
  - Decoupling capacitors (0.1µF ceramic, ~10-20 of them)
  - Breadboard or perfboard for prototyping
  - Jumper wires
